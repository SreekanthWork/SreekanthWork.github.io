An illustrative user story for the broker integration project, with business rules, testable acceptance criteria and full traceability.

US-07

Verify a broker-executed trade against its source signal

Illustrative example · confidential details excluded
User story

As a trading operations analyst,
I want to match every broker execution to its source signal,
so that I can verify trade accuracy before P&L is published.

Business rules
  • A trade is verified only when the signal and execution identifiers, instrument, quantity and side match.
  • Timestamp and price differences must stay within the agreed tolerance; exceptions are flagged for review.
  • Unmatched or incomplete executions are excluded from verified P&L until resolved.
Acceptance criteria
  1. Given a valid source signal and broker execution, when all required fields match, then the trade is marked Verified.
  2. Given a price or timestamp difference exceeds tolerance, when reconciliation runs, then the trade is flagged for review.
  3. Given an execution has no matching signal, when the verification report loads, then it is excluded from verified P&L.
  4. Given a previously flagged mismatch is corrected, when reconciliation reruns, then the status and verified totals update.
