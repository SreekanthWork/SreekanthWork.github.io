What my requirements
look like.

A sample user story with testable acceptance criteria and full traceability, the same structure I use in Jira and Confluence.

US-07

Filter net P&L by date range and instrument

Illustrative example based on the synthetic project
User story

As a trading operations analyst,
I want to filter net P&L by date range and instrument,
so that I can reconcile daily results without manual Excel work.

Business rules
  • Net P&L equals gross P&L minus charges, rounded to 2 decimals.
  • Trades with a missing exit price are flagged as Incomplete and left out of totals.
  • Out of scope: intraday filters and custom instrument groups.
Acceptance criteria
  1. Given trades across 6 instruments, when I choose a date range and one instrument, then only matching trades appear and the totals update.
  2. Given gross P&L and charges are stored, when net P&L is shown, then it equals gross minus charges to 2 decimals.
  3. Given a trade has no exit price, when the report loads, then the trade is marked Incomplete and excluded from totals.
  4. Given no trades match the filters, when the results load, then an empty-state message appears and totals show 0.00.
Story US-07→Requirement FR-07.2→Test case TC-014→UAT: Pass
